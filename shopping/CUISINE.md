## Method & judgment calls

- **Local / Immediate-Regional** = Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Peranakan; local hawker-Chinese (chicken rice, bak kut teh, wanton mee, char kway teow, fishball noodle); Malay/halal-local; **and local-style "Western"** (Astons, Collin's, Han's, kopitiam Western) — per your instruction that Western-Asian is local.
- **East Asian** lumps Korea/Japan/China/Taiwan. **Bubble tea is counted here** (Taiwanese tradition) even for SG operators (KOI, LiHO, Each-a-Cup); new-style Chinese tea/coffee (HeyTea, Mixue, Chagee, Luckin) also here.
- **European/Western** = Italian/French/general Western incl. American fast-food (McDonald's, KFC) and Western coffee (Starbucks, Coffee Bean).
- **Other Asian** = Thai, Vietnamese, Filipino, etc. (beyond the immediate region).
- **Other/Mixed** = Middle Eastern, Mexican, plus origin-agnostic desserts / ice-cream / juice / generic cafes that have no single national cuisine.
- Bakeries/cafes are the fuzziest: classed by brand origin where known (Paris Baguette→Korea, Chateraise→Japan, Tiong Bahru Bakery→French, BreadTalk/Polar/Four Leaves→local), else Other/Mixed.
