# 10 Fun Questions About Singapore Malls

10 questions, answered from the [stores dataset](./data/stores.json).

← [back to shopping](./) · [back to the index](../)

---

## 1. 🛒 Which shop can you literally never escape?

**Watsons** — it's in **39 of 59 malls (66%)**. You are almost never one mall away from a Watsons. F&B equivalent: **Ya Kun Kaya Toast** (32 malls). The whole "heartland mall starter pack" is Watsons + Guardian + a bubble tea wall + Ya Kun.

## 2. 🧋 What's the bubble-tea capital?

**Northpoint City (Yishun)** — **11 bubble-tea stalls** in one mall. Then Suntec (9), Jurong Point & AMK Hub (7). The boba belt is firmly in the heartland.

## 3. 💎 Where's all the bling?

**The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands** — **50 jewellery & watch shops** (≈ a quarter of the entire mall), with **ION Orchard** close behind at 45. MBS is less a mall than a watch-and-jewellery canyon with a casino attached.

## 4. 👯 Which two malls are secretly the same mall?

Closest clones: **Hougang Mall ≈ White Sands**, **Causeway Point ≈ Waterway Point**, **Jurong Point ≈ Northpoint City** — but even the most identical pair only shares **24% of tenants**. So no two malls are true clones; the "cookie-cutter" feeling comes from everyone stocking the same ~20 chains, not from identical line-ups. (Polar opposites: 313@somerset vs Clarke Quay Central / Katong V = **0%** shared tenants.)

## 5. 🍜 Which mall is basically a food court with shops attached?

**One Holland Village** and **AMK Hub** — **47% of their tenants are F&B**. Nearly half of what you walk past is something to eat. (Sengkang Grand, Hougang Mall, Jewel: ~44%.)

## 6. 📚 Where do kiasu parents congregate?

**West Coast Plaza** — **21 enrichment/tuition centres**, a staggering 22% of the whole mall. UOL's **United Square** (the self-styled "Kids Learning Mall") has 15. West Coast Plaza is quietly the tuition capital of Singapore.

## 7. 🦄 Which mall is the least generic?

**Clarke Quay Central** — **74% of its tenants exist in no other mall** in the dataset; **Orchard Central** 69%. Dining/nightlife and Far East's curated malls are where you escape the chains. (The heartland malls are the opposite — ~88% chains-found-elsewhere.)

## 8. ☕ Is Singapore really Starbucks country?

**No.** 23 of 59 malls have **zero Starbucks**, and local kopi (**Ya Kun + Toast Box**) outnumber Starbucks **85 outlets to 36** — better than 2:1. The kopitiam quietly wins the coffee war on home turf.

## 9. 🧟 Which mall would you want to be locked in during the apocalypse?

**Jurong Point** — **24 supermarket + pharmacy/clinic doors** under one roof (FairPrice, Cold Storage, Sheng Siong-adjacent, Watsons, Guardian, plus clinics). Westgate, Northpoint, Tampines 1 and Waterway Point are the runners-up bunkers.

## 10. 🇯🇵 Where's "Little Tokyo"?

**Not Orchard** — **Jurong Point (18 Japanese eateries)**, then **Northpoint City** (17) and **Suntec** (14). The densest Japanese-food cluster in a SG mall is out west in the heartland, not downtown.

---

**Bonus pattern across all 10:** the heartland mega-malls (Jurong Point, Northpoint City, Waterway Point) keep winning the "most X" superlatives — they're simply huge and hyper-standardized, so they top bubble tea, Japanese food, grooming, survival-readiness all at once. The interesting malls are the small distinctive ones (Clarke Quay Central, Orchard Central, West Coast Plaza) that punch above their size on a single quirk.
