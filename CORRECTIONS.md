# Corrections log

A public, append-only record of every correction made to the dataset on request. The goal is twofold:

1. **Demonstrate responsiveness.** Anyone — operator, brand, researcher, or curious visitor — can see that requests are received, considered, and acted on.
2. **Maintain accountability.** Every change to a published record is dated, attributed (where the requester is happy to be named), and citable.

## How a correction gets into this log

- Anyone files a [data-correction issue](https://github.com/curioputterings/singapore-mall-data/issues/new/choose) or emails the maintainer at curioputterings@proton.me.
- We acknowledge within 7 days. If the source can be verified, we update the underlying dataset and republish.
- The change is appended to this file, with the date, the affected record, a short description, and the source.
- The CORRECTIONS row stays in this file in perpetuity, even after later snapshots supersede it.

## Removals (operator opt-outs)

Removal requests follow the same channel but use the [takedown issue template](https://github.com/curioputterings/singapore-mall-data/issues/new/choose). They are unconditional — no reason is required. Acknowledged within 7 days, completed within 30 days. The removal is logged here, naming the entity (or "anonymous, at operator's request" if the requester prefers).
