## Nature of the project

This is an open-data hobby project assembled from **publicly-discoverable sources** at fixed snapshot dates (currently 2026-05-27). It catalogues:

- Singapore shopping centres, their corporate owners, and the shops listed in their public store directories at the snapshot date.
- Nursing / baby-care rooms inside Singapore malls that were findable through operators' own public web pages, publicly-available per-floor coordinate data where it existed, or community-curated directories.

## What the dataset is, and is NOT

**It is** — a descriptive snapshot of what could be observed publicly at a point in time, processed for comparability.

**It is NOT**:

- ❌ an audit, ranking, scorecard, or evaluation of any mall, operator, REIT, retailer, F&B brand, or facility
- ❌ a measurement of physical reality at any mall (signage at the venue itself often shows more than is published online)
- ❌ a measurement of tenant performance, foot traffic, revenue, brand quality, customer experience, or competitive positioning
- ❌ financial advice, investment guidance, valuation, or a recommendation in any context
- ❌ a substitute for due diligence in any commercial, investment, regulatory, or consumer decision
- ❌ an endorsement, criticism, or comparison of any specific operator or brand

## Limitations explicitly acknowledged

- **Tenant directories change frequently.** A store that was listed on the snapshot date may have closed, relocated, or rebranded since.
- **Operator publishing varies.** Some operators publish full directories; others publish curated subsets or do not surface certain fields (e.g. nursing-room capacity) on their digital channels at all.
- **Many derived figures are estimates.** The Financials report flags every value as either D (disclosed by the owner) or E (estimated by us); methodology is documented.
- **Cuisine bucketing and provenance classification are judgement calls** based on publicly-available information. Brands and operators may legitimately disagree with our categorisations.
- **Community-curated sources** (Little Day Out, babyment) used to gap-fill the nursing dataset are known to undercount by 60–90% relative to operator-truth on overlapping malls. They are treated as lower bounds, not counts.
